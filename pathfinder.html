How to read it: BFS and DFS ignore terrain cost entirely — they only care about number of steps, so they'll happily cut through terrain cells. Dijkstra and A* treat terrain cells as 5× more expensive, so they route around them when a cheaper way exists. That difference is the whole point of running them side by side on the same board.
